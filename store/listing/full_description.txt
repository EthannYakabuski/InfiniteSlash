A brave little knight. A very unreasonable number of monsters.

InfiniteSlash is a portrait action slasher and bullet hell made for one hand. Pick Sword or Shield and jump straight into an endless fantasy swarm. Your knight swings automatically. You steer, dodge, guard and turn narrow escapes into ridiculous combos.

ONE HAND, ALL THE CHAOS
Drag to move. Lift your thumb to guard. Flick to dash through trouble. Simple controls get you into the action quickly; timing, positioning and parries give you room to improve every run.

SWORD OR SHIELD?
Sword is fast, fierce and fragile: cut through crowds before they corner you. Shield is slower and much tougher: hold your ground, block the barrage and slash back. Two very different ways to become a very small problem for a very large horde.

100 STRANGE CREATURES
Meet a colorful cast of pixel monsters with names as questionable as their manners. Chargers rush you. Spitters line up shots. Splitters bring friends. Crowned bosses crash the party as the pressure keeps rising.

TURN A GOOD RUN INTO A SILLY ONE
Collect XP for automatic upgrades, chase gold, grab healing hearts and stack temporary powers. Quicksilver speeds things up. Hurricane turns your blade into a crowd-clearing whirlwind. Thunderblade adds sparks to the argument. Keep the combo alive and watch your score climb.

THE ROAD KEEPS GOING
Fight through scrolling meadows, autumn scenery and twilight. Enemy patterns grow more demanding, bosses return, and every little gap becomes a chance to survive a little longer.

ONE MORE RUN
Beat your score, chase survival records, unlock badges and fill your creature collection. Try the daily seeded challenge, or copy a score challenge to send to friends. Falling is one tap away from another attempt.

MADE FOR YOUR POCKET
Portrait play, one-handed controls and quick starts. The core game works offline, saves your progress locally and can resume an unfinished run on the same device.

One thumb. Endless trouble.
