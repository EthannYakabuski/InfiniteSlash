# InfiniteSlash game design

The current version is **0.3.0**, a one-thumb portrait slasher built around automatic attacks, a rechargeable guard, deliberate dashes and paused two-choice run builds.

The [current gameplay specification and A–X acceptance ledger](PRODUCTION_UPDATE.md) covers every mechanic, stage boundary, boss attack, creature cohort, day/night cycle, biome, reward and record. The [build catalog](PRODUCTION_BUILDS.md) details the 103 available upgrades and 72 stat trade-offs.

Player level drives difficulty: 1–29 early, 30–49 the war host, 50–79 Crownfall Keep, 80+ the endgame. Elemental bosses begin at 20; legends can appear at 25. Enemies compound health and damage every ten-level band, while player permanent upgrades grow more gently. Damage, health and guard choices remain useful beyond 50.

Drag to steer, flick to dash, and let the knight swing. Guard absorbs incoming damage during movement or rest and recharges after the class-specific attack-free delay. Dashing through bullets earns Overdrive. Choosing an upgrade pauses the complete combat simulation and requires no extra control during action.

The existing sword/shield class identities, local/offline persistence, separate audio controls, boss collection and Google Play integration are retained. New species milestones and combo/level leaderboards have local tracking and queued online submissions; their Console IDs remain owner-supplied.

Historical mechanics and numbers are preserved in [the 0.2.x design](GAME_DESIGN_0_2.md). They do not describe current guard, progression or population limits.
